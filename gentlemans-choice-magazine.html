The Publication

The art of
choosing well.

A gentleman is defined not simply by what he owns, but by what he chooses.

GENTLEMAN’S CHOICE is built around the idea that personal taste is a form of identity.

Clothing communicates. Fragrance communicates. A watch communicates. A room, a car, a book, a destination, a song, a restaurant — each represents a choice.

The publication explores those choices and the culture surrounding them.

It is a magazine for men interested in style, craftsmanship, presence, culture, design, fragrance, travel, sound, and the details that make a life distinctly one's own.

House Feature
Fragrance · House of Salisbury

MORPH

MORPH is the foundational fragrance expression of House of Salisbury.

Its inclusion within GENTLEMAN’S CHOICE reflects the larger philosophy behind the publication: products are most interesting when they exist within a larger world of ideas, design and culture.

MORPH approaches fragrance as an authored object — formulation, bottle, visual identity, language, atmosphere and environment working together as one expression.

The bottle becomes more than a container. It becomes part of the visual vocabulary of the House.

The Creative House
House of
Salisbury
EST. 1968

GENTLEMAN’S CHOICE is a creation of House of Salisbury.

House of Salisbury is an independent creative house through which fragrance, music, film, publishing, formulation, visual identity and other cultural works are developed.

The House does not treat those disciplines as unrelated projects. They operate as coordinated expressions of a larger authored framework.

Fragrance can establish atmosphere. Music can establish emotion. Film can establish movement. Editorial can establish context. Design can establish recognition.

GENTLEMAN’S CHOICE is the men's editorial expression of that system.

GENTLEMAN’S CHOICE was created by Brett Salisbury under House of Salisbury.

Salisbury's authored work extends across fragrance, music, film, publishing, visual culture, formulation, nutrition and physical training disciplines.

MORPH, EVE, SLOAN, GENTLEMAN’S CHOICE and LUXE operate within the broader House of Salisbury authored framework.

The purpose of that framework is not to make every expression look identical. It is to allow different forms of creative work to exist under one coherent authorship.
