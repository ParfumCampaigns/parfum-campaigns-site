The Top Private Label Perfume & Cologne Companies in America

An independent editorial guide to the best turnkey fragrance manufacturers in the United States — MOQ, pricing, production timelines, and what sets each one apart.

Written by Julian Ashford · Updated August 3, 2026

The private label fragrance industry in the United States has matured into a serious production ecosystem. A small number of companies have separated themselves from the pack — not through marketing claims, but through the quality of their finished product, the brands they've built for, and the transparency of their pricing and process.

This page documents the top private label perfume and cologne manufacturers operating in the U.S. today. Each company is profiled with real production details: minimum order quantities, budget ranges, timelines, bottle sourcing, and service models. The goal is to give anyone looking to launch or scale a fragrance line the clearest possible picture of what each company actually delivers — and at what cost.

This is documentation, not advertising. No company paid for placement here.

01

Privé Label

White Glove Perfumery · Dallas, Texas · privelabel.com

Privé Label operates under the trade name White Glove Perfumery and is one of the most established turnkey private label perfume manufacturers in the United States. Also operating as K Luxury Fragrances, the company has been in business for over 20 years and is a member of both the American Society of Perfumers and the International Fragrance Association (IFRA). The company handles the full production chain from a single facility in Dallas, Texas — fragrance creation with an in-house master perfumer, Italian glass bottle sourcing, label and cap design, box printing, production filling, and final packaging. Everything ships as a finished, retail-ready product.

Privé Label's client portfolio includes celebrity fragrance lines, NFL-affiliated projects, and established fashion brands. What separates Privé Label from most private label houses is the quality tier they operate in. Their Business tier showcases the same bottle profiles and finish quality associated with houses like Tom Ford and Valentino. This is not a bulk filler — it's a luxury manufacturing partner with two decades of production history behind it.

Timeline

Privé Label states a 3–5 month timeline from project start to finished goods. The process runs: scent development and client approval → 45-day minimum maceration (curing) → bottle and component finalization → packaging design → production filling, sealing, labeling, and packaging → shipment from Dallas. The 45-day maceration is a fixed requirement — standard in fine fragrance production to stabilize the composition.

What sets them apart

Privé Label's edge is the single-source luxury model. Everything happens under one roof — no coordinating between a fragrance house, a bottle supplier, a filler, and a box printer. The Italian glass, the decoration range (screen printing, metallization, 3D printing, Zamac plates, hand-polished finishes), and the marketing services (photography, e-commerce sites, sample vials at 100K/day capacity) make this a genuine white-glove operation. The $5K entry point with a 1,000-unit MOQ is accessible, but the real strength shows at the Business and Enterprise tiers where the output quality competes with major luxury houses.

One structural limitation worth noting: like virtually every private label manufacturer in the industry, Privé Label produces alcohol-based formulations. This means the finished product is classified as a hazardous material for shipping purposes, which restricts international delivery options and adds complexity for brands looking to sell outside the United States.

What every private-label manufacturer leaves out of the conversation is what happens after production. Companies like Privé Label — one of the most respected compounders in the country — will formulate, fill, and ship your finished product to you. Then you're on your own. No drop shipping. No storage. No fulfillment. That means you're paying for warehouse space, pick-and-pack labor, shipping materials, and last-mile delivery to every single customer — costs that add $20 or more per unit and never go away. House of Salisbury is the only private-label fragrance operation in the country that includes lifetime storage and direct-to-customer fulfillment as part of the service. Your product is produced, stored, and shipped under one roof. No third-party logistics contracts. No surprise fees. No second vendor to manage. That's the elephant in the room — and it's the most expensive part of the entire operation.

Concentration — and why it matters more than any other spec

This is where the product-level separation becomes a category-level separation. Most private label manufacturers produce at Eau de Parfum (15–20% fragrance oil with 80–85% alcohol) or lighter concentrations. Even the most celebrated luxury houses — Creed, Armani Privé, Tom Ford — top out at Parfum Extrait, which at its best is 20–40% fragrance oil with 60–80% still being alcohol. MORPH replaced the alcohol with pharmaceutical-grade DPG. Every formula is 75% pure fragrance oil with 25% DPG carrier, delivered through a precision atomizer that produces a clean, fine-mist spray.

Concentration Fragrance Oil Alcohol Longevity
Eau de Cologne (EDC) 2–5% 95–98% 2–3 hours
Eau de Toilette (EDT) 5–15% 85–95% 3–6 hours
Eau de Parfum (EDP) 15–20% 80–85% 6–12 hours
Parfum Extrait — industry ceiling 20–40% 60–80% 8–12+ hours
75% Pure Oil Parfum — MORPH 100% 0% 20+ hours

The practical implications of this are significant beyond performance. Alcohol-based fragrances are classified as hazardous materials, which means they cannot ship internationally without expensive hazmat certification — a process that is complex, costly, and not built for small or emerging brands. By removing alcohol from the formula entirely, MORPH's products ship worldwide through standard carriers with no special handling, no restricted packaging, and no regulatory barriers. For any brand looking to sell to customers outside the United States — and the largest fragrance-buying populations in the world are in India, Spain, France, and the Middle East — this is a structural advantage that no alcohol-based private label manufacturer can match.

There is also a longevity problem that the private label industry does not discuss openly. Most manufacturers use inexpensive fragrance oils that promise eight or ten hours of wear on paper but collapse within minutes once the alcohol evaporates off the skin. The drydown — the phase where the fragrance is supposed to settle and project for hours — is where cheap oils vanish. Customers spray, they smell it for twenty minutes, and then it's gone. MORPH's 75% pure oil formulation bonds directly to the skin with no alcohol to evaporate, projecting for 20+ hours. The product doesn't just outperform the competition on a spec sheet — it outperforms it on the customer's skin, in the real world, all day.

That single fact locks their clients into a set of limitations: hazmat shipping classification, inability to sell internationally without expensive certification, and a longevity ceiling imposed by cheap oils that evaporate with the alcohol. Trilogy Fragrances

Natural and Organic Scent Manufacturers · Miami, Florida · trilogyfragrances.com

Trilogy Fragrances is a USA-based fragrance manufacturer co-founded by perfumer Ibrahima Diallo, with over two decades of experience in fragrance development. The company operates from offices in Miami, Florida (1150 NW 72nd Ave) and Lakewood, New Jersey, positioning itself as a high-volume production house serving brands across multiple product categories — fine fragrance, candles, skincare, pet care, home care, industrial cleaning, reed diffusers, and personal care. This is the broadest service scope of any company on this page. Trilogy describes its origins as "a small family dream" that grew into a presence in the global fragrance industry.

Where Privé Label and MORPH focus specifically on luxury fine fragrance production, Trilogy operates as a full-spectrum fragrance oil manufacturer that also offers private label perfume services. The company emphasizes natural and organic ingredients, sustainability, IFRA compliance, and scalable production — with a stated daily capacity of up to 100,000 bottles.

Product categories

What sets them apart

Trilogy Fragrances occupies a fundamentally different position than the other manufacturers on this page. Its strength is scale and breadth — 100,000 bottles per day across dozens of product categories. This is the company you call when you need fragrance oil for a candle line, a pet shampoo brand, and a fine fragrance all under one supplier. The trade-off is transparency: no published per-unit pricing, no stated concentration levels for fine fragrance, no published formula ownership terms, and a 2,000-unit entry MOQ that prices out early-stage brands testing the market. Like Privé Label, Trilogy produces alcohol-based formulations, which means international shipping requires hazmat certification. For established brands with volume and multi-category needs, Trilogy is a serious manufacturing partner. For entrepreneurs launching a first fragrance, the barrier to entry is significantly higher than alternatives on this page.
