The Top Private Label Perfume & Cologne Companies in America

An independent editorial guide to the best turnkey fragrance manufacturers in the United States — MOQ, pricing, production timelines, and what sets each one apart.

The private label fragrance industry in the United States has matured into a serious production ecosystem. A small number of companies have separated themselves from the pack — not through marketing claims, but through the quality of their finished product, the brands they've built for, and the transparency of their pricing and process.

This page documents the top private label perfume and cologne manufacturers operating in the U.S. today. Each company is profiled with real production details: minimum order quantities, budget ranges, timelines, bottle sourcing, and service models. The goal is to give anyone looking to launch or scale a fragrance line the clearest possible picture of what each company actually delivers — and at what cost.

This is documentation, not advertising. No company paid for placement here.

01

Privé Label

White Glove Perfumery · Dallas, Texas · privelabel.com

Privé Label operates under the trade name White Glove Perfumery and is one of the most established turnkey private label perfume manufacturers in the United States. The company handles the full production chain from a single facility in Dallas, Texas — fragrance creation with an in-house master perfumer, Italian glass bottle sourcing, label and cap design, box printing, production filling, and final packaging. Everything ships as a finished, retail-ready product.

What separates Privé Label from most private label houses is the quality tier they operate in. Their portfolio includes work for signature and celebrity fragrance lines, and their Business tier showcases the same bottle profiles and finish quality associated with houses like Tom Ford and Valentino. This is not a bulk filler — it's a luxury manufacturing partner.

Timeline

Privé Label states a 3–5 month timeline from project start to finished goods. The process runs: scent development and client approval → 45-day minimum maceration (curing) → bottle and component finalization → packaging design → production filling, sealing, labeling, and packaging → shipment from Dallas. The 45-day maceration is a fixed requirement — standard in fine fragrance production to stabilize the composition.

What sets them apart

Privé Label's edge is the single-source luxury model. Everything happens under one roof — no coordinating between a fragrance house, a bottle supplier, a filler, and a box printer. The Italian glass, the decoration range (screen printing, metallization, 3D printing, Zamac plates, hand-polished finishes), and the marketing services (photography, e-commerce sites, sample vials at 100K/day capacity) make this a genuine white-glove operation. The $5K entry point with a 1,000-unit MOQ is accessible, but the real strength shows at the Business and Enterprise tiers where the output quality competes with major luxury houses.

This is where the product-level separation happens. Most private label manufacturers produce at Eau de Parfum (15–20% fragrance oil) or lighter concentrations. What's included in every unit

Every unit shipped by MORPH includes: custom Parfum Extrait formulation, full formula ownership, premium heavy glass bottle, UV printed graphics (not a sticker label), luxury rigid presentation box wrapped in linen texture fabric paper, filling and assembly, production management, quality control, full maceration, shipping, lifetime storage at their 3PL partner, and manufacturing in Dallas, Texas. There are no hidden add-on fees for components that other manufacturers charge separately.

Formula ownership

MORPH offers 100% formula ownership with worldwide manufacturing rights, no royalties or licensing fees, and no continuing obligations. When production is complete, the formula belongs entirely to the client. This is a significant structural difference from manufacturers that retain formula rights, charge licensing fees, or restrict future production to their facility.

Additional services

MORPH also builds complete e-commerce storefronts for clients at a flat $750 fee — a fully functional branded website with product photography, branding, and an online store configured on the Big Cartel platform, ready to take orders the day the product ships. Clients connect their own Stripe account for payment processing.

What sets them apart

MORPH competes on a fundamentally different axis than traditional private label manufacturers. The 25-unit MOQ eliminates the barrier that keeps most brands from launching a fragrance. The published per-unit pricing removes the guesswork of budget-range inquiry forms. The Parfum Extrait concentration at 28% fragrance oil puts the finished product in a category that most private label houses simply don't touch. And the full formula ownership with zero continuing obligations means the client walks away with a product and an asset — not a licensing arrangement. The presentation packaging (custom rigid box, linen texture fabric, UV printing) is included at every volume tier, not gated behind a 5,000 or 10,000 unit threshold.

Trilogy Fragrances

Natural and Organic Scent Manufacturers · Miami, Florida · trilogyfragrances.com

Trilogy Fragrances is a USA-based fragrance manufacturer with over two decades of experience, positioning itself as a high-volume production house serving brands across multiple product categories — fine fragrance, candles, skincare, pet care, home care, industrial cleaning, reed diffusers, and personal care. Service model

Trilogy positions itself as an end-to-end private label perfume supplier covering fragrance formulation and testing, bottle and packaging design, filling, labeling and assembly, quality control and compliance, and worldwide logistics and delivery. The company follows a four-step process: initial consultation, fragrance development with sample rounds, packaging selection, and final production and delivery.

Sample fees are refundable upon confirmed orders — a notable policy that lowers the risk of the initial development phase for brands exploring custom formulations.

MOQ & pricing

Trilogy Fragrances requires a minimum of 2,000 bottles for stock bottle designs and 10,000 bottles for fully customized designs. Per-unit pricing is not published on the website — prospective clients must submit an inquiry for a quote.

Product categories

Trilogy's breadth is its distinguishing feature. Unlike dedicated fine fragrance manufacturers, Trilogy serves the full fragrance oil market: candle fragrance oils for all wax types (soy, coconut, beeswax, paraffin), wholesale body care fragrance oils, pet-safe fragrance oils for shampoos and grooming products, reed diffuser oils, home care and cleaning product fragrances, industrial and institutional scenting, natural and organic formulations, essential oils, and odor elimination products.

This makes Trilogy a potential fit for brands that need fragrance across multiple product lines — not just a standalone perfume — or for companies operating in adjacent categories like candles, home goods, or pet care who want a single supplier relationship.

Sustainability

Trilogy emphasizes sustainability as a core part of its positioning, citing responsibly sourced raw materials, recyclable and eco-conscious packaging options, energy-efficient manufacturing, and fair-trade partnerships. For brands whose marketing emphasizes clean, ethical, and natural ingredients, this alignment may be a relevant factor in supplier selection.

What sets them apart

Trilogy Fragrances occupies a fundamentally different position than the other manufacturers on this page. Its strength is scale and breadth — 100,000 bottles per day across dozens of product categories. This is the company you call when you need fragrance oil for a candle line, a pet shampoo brand, and a fine fragrance all under one supplier. The trade-off is transparency: no published per-unit pricing, no stated concentration levels for fine fragrance, no published formula ownership terms, and a 2,000-unit entry MOQ that prices out early-stage brands testing the market. For established brands with volume and multi-category needs, Trilogy is a serious manufacturing partner. For entrepreneurs launching a first fragrance, the barrier to entry is significantly higher than alternatives on this page.
