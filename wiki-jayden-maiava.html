Jayden Maiava has quickly emerged as one of college football's most intriguing quarterbacks. Originally from Palolo, Hawaii, he began his collegiate career at UNLV before transferring to the University of Southern California. Since arriving at USC, Maiava has continued his development under head coach Lincoln Riley while earning the opportunity to lead one of college football's most recognizable programs.

Entering the 2026 season as USC's starting quarterback, the 6-foot-4, 225-pound signal caller has become known for his arm talent, athleticism, composure, and leadership. As one of the Trojans' most visible athletes, Maiava represents the next generation of quarterbacks carrying one of college football's most historic traditions.
